AI Playground for Climate Action

How organizations working on climate and the environment have used AI, told in depth, with something you can try for yourself in each story.

The Playground is a space on the Hub where you can read the stories of environmental initiatives that have used AI, try something for yourself and talk to others doing similar work. Each story follows one initiative's journey, from the first idea to where the tool stands now, including what did not work and what they decided not to build.

The stories come from interviews and case studies with organizations in The Engine Room's AI Collaborative. Each one comes with a short walk-through: you do, step by step, what the people in the story do, with example material, and come away understanding one real decision the initiative made. Stories come first; more ways to experiment will follow over time.

This is an early version. Every walk-through uses example data that we wrote or drew for this purpose. Nothing comes from the initiatives' own systems, and nothing you type or choose here leaves your browser.

Three ways of looking at each story

We read every story through the three lenses of the framework we are developing with the AI Collaborative. Each story leads with one of them.

  • Community-centered: whose needs it serves, who decides and whose knowledge counts.
  • Frugal: whether a smaller, local or lighter option was tried or would have worked.
  • Ecological: effects on communities, territories and ecosystems, not only energy use.

The stories

Frugal

Report a flood, then see it on the map

PetaBencana, Indonesia, with a sister platform in the Philippines. People report floods from where they stand, in the terms they already use, and their reports appear on a public map.

Follow one example report from a social media post to the map, and set its depth by ankle, knee or waist.

Community-centered

Photograph a plant so a model can learn from it

Taxonomy meets Tech, Lebanon. A university team and a biosphere reserve built a local plant dataset, FloraLebanon, with student volunteers.

Learn the photo protocol the volunteers followed, then sort example photos the way the team's experts did.

Start the walk-through

Ecological

From photo to diagnosis, confidence and an expert

Crop disease detection in Ghana. An app that diagnoses crop disease from a photo, built with farmers, which had to change when the rains stopped.

Follow an example leaf photo through a diagnosis and decide when to ask an expert.

About the material

The initiatives agreed to share their case studies on the Hub. Screenshots of their tools, samples of their real data and links to live systems need a further conversation with each of them, so where a walk-through would use one of those, you will see a note saying it is still to confirm. We name initiatives, not individuals. Quotes come from the case studies.
